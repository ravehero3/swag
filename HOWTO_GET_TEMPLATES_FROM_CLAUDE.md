## After Claude Writes The Templates

### Step 1: Save The Templates
- Copy all 12 templates into a document or Notion
- Organize by journey

### Step 2: Add Them To Your System
1. Go to **Admin > Marketing > "Šablony"** (Templates) in your app
2. Click **"+ Nová šablona"** for each template
3. Paste the email body
4. Set the template name to match (e.g., "free_beat_onboarding_day3")

### Step 3: Link To Journeys
1. Go to **Admin > Marketing > Journeys**
2. Click the journey (e.g., "Free Beat Onboarding")
3. For each step, select the corresponding template
4. Save

### Step 4: Test
1. Go to flow builder for the journey
2. Enter your test email in the "Testovací e-mail" field
3. Click the "Send Test" button on a step
4. Check your email inbox
5. Verify layout, links, personalization, etc.

### Step 5: Activate
1. Journeys start in `draft` status (won't send)
2. When ready, click **Activate** on the journey
3. Now emails will send automatically when triggered

### Step 6: Monitor
1. Check **Analytika** (Analytics) in Marketing tab
2. Track open rates, click rates, conversions
3. Optimize based on performance
